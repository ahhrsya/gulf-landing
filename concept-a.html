Questions

What prospective operators need to know.

What kind of venue is Creative Park?

It is a wellness and fitness-led commercial destination that brings sports, wellbeing and curated lifestyle concepts into one place.

Who is Creative Park best suited for?

It is designed for wellness brands, fitness operators, sports concepts, curated cafés and lifestyle-driven businesses.

What makes it different from standard commercial space?

The concept puts greater emphasis on walkability, nature, community and the everyday experience of the destination.

Can food and beverage brands take space?

Yes. Curated F&B concepts are part of the intended venue mix and support its wider wellness and lifestyle environment.
